# Production-readiness audit — 23 October 2026

**Scope:**
- the whole monorepo: API, worker, console, mobile, migrations and CI;
- the platform document `sideqwest-complete-platform.md`;
- the Sell-the-Concept kit;
- the project's earlier audits (19 and 23 October, the v0 Vercel app).

**Method:**
- read every server-side path that touches money, identity, location or safety;
- trace every retry, timer, poll and reconnect to its bound;
- check each document claim against the code.

**Verification:** every fix ships with a test in `tests/e2e/hardening.test.ts` or a package
unit test. CI runs them against Postgres with PostGIS and h3, and Redis, as the RLS-bound
roles.

The decisions these findings forced are in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). The corrected
platform description is [PLATFORM.md](PLATFORM.md).

Severity: **Critical** means money lost or a remote compromise is likely in production.
**High** means money or safety is at risk under conditions that will happen. **Medium** is a
real weakness with limited impact. **Low** is hygiene.

Three review findings were checked and left as they are: the feed already measures to the cell
centre (`h3_cell_to_geometry` returns the centroid; the feed test reads its coordinates with
`ST_Y`/`ST_X`, which fail on a polygon); the watchdog's lock key is the ISO time to the tens of
minutes (`slice(0, 15)` is `2026-10-23T09:1`), so it runs once per ten minutes; and a refresh
race across console instances needs a deployment RELEASE.md already rules out (one replica, or
sticky sessions), where it signed officers out before this PR too.

## Checked and found sound

- **Outbox:** claim, lease and park, at most 10 attempts with backoff. BullMQ job retries:
  5, exponential.
- **Decline ladder:** bounded by its rungs. The reimbursement window has its own wake.
- **Expiry jobs:** offer, errand, auction and reimbursement expiries re-check the clock and
  state before acting.
- **Pollers and reconnects:** react-query polling stops on status changes. Mobile socket
  backoff is capped at 30 s. Refresh is single-flight on both clients.
- **Concurrency:** assignment races, cap races (SERIALIZABLE) and the withdrawal and payout
  locks.
- **Input handling:** webhook source and token gate, callback matching, upload signing and
  path containment, and the CSV formula guard on exports.
- **RLS:** coverage gates, the append-only ledger and column-level grants on `account`.
