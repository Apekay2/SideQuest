Report a problem

What happened?

Opening a report freezes the escrow and the Qwest card. The money sits still — it protects you and it protects your runner.

Who decides

Side Qwest Legal Operations rules on the case using the evidence the errand already collected: item photos, card charges, receipts, the handover PIN and its location stamp. Their decision releases, splits or refunds the escrow, and it binds both sides.

Legal Operations answers within 24 hours, and either side can ask for one review. In danger right now? Use SOS on the live Qwest screen — it calls 999 and shares your location and runner's verified identity.
