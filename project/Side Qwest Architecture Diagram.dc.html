Money invariants
Runner never fronts cash — the only spendable instrument is the errand's own card.
The card holds nothing until proof arrives, and stops existing when the errand closes.
A tranche loads for one requester-approved vendor leg only; unspent float returns to the requester.
Money the requester settles at the till is never counted as card spend — it is reimbursed from escrow at close.
Batched errands share a trip, never a card, an escrow or an approval.
Every shilling is a balanced posting pair; corrections are reversals, never edits.
A report freezes escrow and voids the card, protecting both sides equally.
Only Legal Operations can split or reverse a frozen escrow, and rulings bind both parties.
